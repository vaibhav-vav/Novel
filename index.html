The Author

Vaibhav Singh

Novelist  ·  Storyteller  ·  Visionary

Hey everyone, I'm Vaibhav Singh Raghuvanshi. I write novels, and build fictional worlds that pull you right into the heart of the story. My book, "Beast Breakdown," drops you straight into a medieval fantasy loaded with magic, power plays, and battles that actually mean something. This world isn't just for show—every choice matters, every alliance changes things, and, honestly, the battles have their own stories worth remembering. So, if you're up for something beyond the usual, I invite you in. Mystery, adventure, and a heavy dose of imagination are waiting. The world of Beast Breakdown is ready. All you have to do is turn the page and dive in.

Beast Breakdown marks the beginning of an ambitious novel series that promises to redefine the dark fantasy genre — one visceral chapter at a time.
